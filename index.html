FEATURED · THE TONGA OF KARIBA

The Tonga: people of the great river

The Tonga (BaTonga) have lived along the Zambezi for centuries, in the Binga and Kariba area of the Zambezi Valley. When Lake Kariba was filled in the 1950s, thousands were resettled, and the river spirit Nyaminyami remains central to their stories. Today, Tonga hosts share their culture with visitors.

  • Binga basket weaving: hand-woven baskets in coiled grass and palm
  • Ngoma buntibe: traditional drum and dance gatherings
  • Fishing the lake in dugout-style boats, and kapenta fishing at dusk
  • Storytelling about Nyaminyami and the river
